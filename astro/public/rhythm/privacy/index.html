Rhythm — Privacy Policy

Published by ByteScribble · Effective 20 October 2026

Rhythm works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no server and no internet access, and we do not collect, receive or share any of your data.

What Rhythm stores

To work, Rhythm saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • the activities in your routine (names, times, days and icons);
  • your check-ins (whether you finished, partially finished or skipped an activity);
  • your settings, such as reminder preferences.

This information never leaves your phone through Rhythm. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Rhythm contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking. We cannot see your routine, your check-ins or anything else you enter.

Notifications

Rhythm asks for permission to show notifications and to schedule reminders at exact times, so it can remind you when an activity starts and ask whether you finished it. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in Rhythm's settings or in your phone's settings at any time; the rest of the app keeps working.

Device backups

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your phone settings, Android may include Rhythm's data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, as it does for other apps. That backup is managed by Google under your account, not by us, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

You can erase all of your data from Rhythm's settings. Uninstalling the app also removes everything it stored on your phone.

Children

Rhythm is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect any from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If Rhythm ever starts collecting data, we will say so here before that version is released.
